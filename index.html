Common Questions

How many people does it sleep?
The Bali Dome has two bedrooms with luxury bedding and sleeps up to 4 guests comfortably. Perfect for couples, friends traveling together, or small families seeking a nature escape without sacrificing space.
Is it really private?
Yes. The entire 850 sq ft dome, 400 sq ft patio, and surrounding area are exclusively yours during your stay. You won't see or hear other guests. The property sits on 15 secluded acres with no neighbors in sight.
How far is it from Dallas?
About 90 minutes by car from downtown Dallas. The drive itself is part of the experience—watching the city fade into farmland, then forest.
Is there really high-speed internet in the woods?
Yes. Starlink satellite internet delivers high-speed connectivity throughout the property—fast enough for streaming 4K, video calls, or remote work. Unplug if you want to, not because you have to.
What's included in the outdoor space?
Your private 400 sq ft teak patio features one-of-a-kind furniture (including that 9-foot acacia sofa carved from a single slab), Solo Stove fire pit, hot tub, outdoor kitchen, and hammock. The property also includes 300 feet of string lights, walking trails, a natural pond, and an 18-hole disc golf course winding through the pines.
Can we cook?
Absolutely. The dome includes a fully stocked gourmet kitchen with everything you need—cookware, utensils, coffee maker, retro designer fridge, and more. There's also an outdoor kitchen on the patio for al fresco dining under the string lights.
What if the weather is bad?
The dome is climate-controlled year-round with heating and AC. Rain on the 20-foot ceiling is often guests' favorite part—it's surprisingly peaceful. Storms create the best stargazing afterward through the electric retractable sunroof.
What makes this "Bali-inspired"?
The architecture borrows from Balinese design: open vaulted ceilings, natural materials (teak and acacia imported from Indonesia), floor-to-ceiling views of nature, and an emphasis on indoor-outdoor flow. It's tropical luxury design adapted for the Texas landscape—surrounded by pine, juniper, and oak instead of palms.
Can we see the stars from inside?
Yes. The 20-foot vaulted ceiling features a retractable electric sunroof. Open it at night and stargaze from bed. The 18-foot bay windows also give you an unobstructed forest view—no light pollution for miles.
Are there bugs or wildlife?
You're in nature on 15 wooded acres. You may see insects, deer, birds, or other animals outside. The dome itself is clean, sealed, and professionally maintained. Many guests appreciate seeing wildlife—it's part of what makes this special.
Do you allow pets?
We love pets! However, we keep the Bali Dome allergen-free so all guests can breathe easy. This means no pets inside the dome. If you're traveling with a furry friend, we're happy to recommend nearby pet-friendly accommodations.
What if we need to grab something?
Paris is 10–15 minutes away with grocery stores, restaurants, gas stations, and everything else you might need. But most guests stock up before arriving and never leave the property—that's kind of the point.
